Research

Research themes: modelling, estimation, control, perception and sensing for marine robots.

Our themes follow the chain from physics to behavior: model the vehicle, estimate its state, control it, and sense the water around it.

1. Underwater vehicle modelling and control

How can we develop compact, full-stack 6-DoF ROVs with reliable non-linear dynamics for precise operations in confined coastal environments?

Platform: Marjan-R1 ROV · Status: Integrated prototype validated in controlled pool experiments · Researchers: Dr. Sarvat Ahmad, Dr. Ali AlBeladi, Syed Imtiaz Ali Shah, Masroor Khan

Current work and results We have built and modeled a nonlinear six-degree-of-freedom ROV. The vehicle acts as an experimental platform for system identification and closed-loop control. We recently completed open-loop pool validation, confirming static buoyancy trim and demonstrating strong coupling between axes, which motivates our ongoing closed-loop control development.

2. Energy-efficient underwater autonomy

How can autonomous underwater gliders coordinate shared actuators to achieve precise heading changes while descending on a limited energy budget?

Platform: Underwater glider / SABR · Status: Modeled and simulated; hardware in development · Researchers: Dr. Ali AlBeladi, Julio Zevallos, Dr. Abdelhadi Aouaichia

Current work and results We have developed a turn-coordinated active disturbance rejection control (TC-ADRC) architecture for underactuated gliders. The kinematic turn coordinator limits the commanded sink rate so that the relative flow driving the turn lasts as long as the heading task requires.

3. Bioinspired marine robotics

How can we identify and control the coupled heading dynamics of bioinspired fish-like robots driven entirely by internal reaction wheels?

Platform: Bioinspired fish-like robots · Status: Hardware prototype validated in pool trials · Researchers: Joshymar A. Echeverria Ortiz, Dr. Ali AlBeladi, Abdullah Alavi

Current work and results We performed system identification of heading dynamics for a NACA-0040 internal-rotor fishlike robot directly from pool trial data. Using Parametric Weak-Form Sparse Regression (P-WSINDyc), we isolated the specific dynamic term responsible for heading drift under asymmetric actuation, providing a data-driven basis for future control design.

4. Underwater perception and state estimation

How can we accurately estimate the state of a compact marine vehicle by fusing low-cost onboard sensors in real-time?

Platform: Marjan-R1 ROV & Pose-estimation system · Status: In development & Pool testing · Researchers: Dr. Ali AlBeladi, Syed Imtiaz Ali Shah

Current work and results We are fusing inertial, pressure, and sonar data, and fitting model parameters from experiments. We have successfully applied an Unscented Kalman Filter (UKF) for real-time hydrodynamic parameter estimation on Marjan-R1 during pool tests, allowing for real-time state feedback.

5. Marine and environmental sensing

How can spectral and geometric sensing identify materials, surfaces and environmental properties beyond conventional RGB imaging?

Platform: Benchtop hyperspectral imaging rig · Status: Laboratory system validated on the bench; not yet integrated with an underwater vehicle · Researchers: Dr. Ali AlBeladi

Current work and results Development of an open-source pushbroom HSI platform and depth-aware HSI–RGB-D fusion for material association and 3D mapping.

Technical details

6. Simulation and digital twins

How can we test advanced controllers safely and efficiently before deployment in physical hardware trials?

Platform: Simulation and digital-twin environments · Status: Active · Researchers: Dr. Abdelhadi Aouaichia, Julio Zevallos, Dr. Ali AlBeladi

Current work and results Simulation serves as the shared infrastructure supporting the other programs. We employ a replicable simulation-to-test framework using Gazebo/ROS 2 combined with CFD inputs to evaluate energy-aware control and disturbance rejection under simulated currents and noise.
