Marjan-R1 ROV

Integrated prototype — validated in controlled pool experiments

An inspection-class remotely operated vehicle with eight vectored thrusters, designed and built in-house.

Specification

PurposeInspection and station keeping in controlled water; research platform for control and system identification.
Current statusIntegrated prototype validated in controlled pool experiments. No open-water operation yet.
PropulsionEight vectored thrusters, six degrees of freedom
Dimensions567 × 324 × 352 mm
Mass14.5 kg, neutrally buoyant
Design depth rating100 m [TO CONFIRM basis]
Power and linkTethered power and communication; video and telemetry to a ground station
SpeedUp to 2 knots
DemonstratedHydrostatic trim, thruster control, open-loop step responses in a pool
Test environmentLaboratory pool
